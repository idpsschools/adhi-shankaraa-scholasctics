About Adhi Shankaraa Scholastics

Adhi Shankaraa Scholastics is an educational services and licensing company headquartered in Ponnapalli, Cherukupalli, in Bapatla district, Andhra Pradesh. The company provides scholastic, certification, documentation, and intellectual-property licensing services to educational institutions. Its activities include facilitating the authorized use of educational trademarks and associated brand assets, supporting institutional requirements through structured licensing arrangements, and managing applicable royalty and related commercial arrangements. In association with International Delhi Public School, Cherukupalli, Adhi Shankaraa Scholastics provides the relevant trademark licensing and institutional support framework under which the school operates.
