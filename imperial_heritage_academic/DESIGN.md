## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the stately gravitas, intellectual heritage, and ceremonial dignity of an elite Indian educational society. The aesthetic merges traditional academic prestige with modern clarity—evoking architectural stone seals, brass-embossed certificates, and venerable library halls, while maintaining exceptional functional legibility across digital portals and institutional touchpoints.

### Key Tenets
- **Regal Authority:** Grounded in sovereign purple and burnished gold, expressing time-tested stewardship and scholarly excellence.
- **Academic Restraint:** Generous negative space, structured layouts, and warm parchment surfaces reject fleeting trends in favor of timeless composure.
- **Harmonized Tradition:** High-contrast editorial serif display elements pair with a meticulous, neutral sans-serif core for effortless information hierarchy.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Warm-tinted surfaces and whisper-thin hairline gold/slate borders emulate heavy vellum, fine bookbinding, and formal charter documentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of stature through royal jewel tones counterpoised with luminous warm vellum backgrounds.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Imperial Purple (`#3B1259` / `#4A154B`):** The commanding anchor. Reserved for institutional seals, primary headers, high-impact cards, navigation bars, and primary CTAs.
- **Secondary Sovereign Gold (`#C89620` / `#D97706`):** Symbolizes enlightenment and scholastic achievement. Used purposefully for heraldic badges, active indicators, borders of distinction, key metrics, and celebratory highlights. Never used for long-form reading text.
- **Parchment Surfaces (`#FCFAF7` Base, `#F6F1EA` Elevated, `#EDE5D8` Border Subtle):** Replaces clinical cool whites with rich, natural manuscript tones that soften reading strain and evoke fine stationery.
- **Deep Slate Charcoal (`#1E1A22` Primary Ink, `#4A4350` Secondary Ink, `#766D7D` Muted Ink):** Delivers uncompromising contrast and readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Status & Semantics:**
  - Success / Honor: Deep Emerald (`#1B5E3C`)
  - Notice / Alert: Warm Ochre (`#B45309`)
  - Critical / Formal Warning: Crimson Claret (`#8B1D2C`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the monumental elegance of **Playfair Display** for editorial presence with the crystalline precision of **Inter** for data tables, administration modules, syllabi, and administrative portals.

### Typographic Rules
- **Editorial Ceremonial Display:** `Playfair Display` provides stately weight to landing banners, institutional manifestos, and section headers. High-ranking headings use subtle optical tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ensure authority.
- **Body & Operations:** `Inter` handles informational density, student transcripts, forms, and general communication with high legibility and relaxed line-heights.
- **Honorific Sub-headers & Metadata:** `label-md` and `label-sm` frequently use all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) when introducing institutional programs, accreditations, and dates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural 12-column grid system resting on an 8px rhythmic foundation, prioritizing balanced symmetry and structured cadence.

### Grid & Breakpoint Specifications
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `2rem` (32px) gutters, generous margins scaling up to a max-width container of `1280px`. Institutional dashboards can extend to `1440px` with fixed 32px margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` side margins. Dual-column layouts for split information modules and academic profiles.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` edge margins. Elements reflow into stacked cards and sequential single-column flows.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- Spacing embraces classical pacing: ample vertical margins (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) separate major academic councils, historical milestones, and faculty directories.
- Inner card and form padding stays compact and consistent (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), ensuring an efficient balance between prestige and administrative density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **tonal parchment layering**, **hairline structural borders**, and **warm ambient shadows**, strictly avoiding sterile tech-oriented blue dropshadows.

### Architectural Layering Principles
- **Base Canvas:** Deep parchment `#FCFAF7` serves as the foundational level.
- **Elevated Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F6F1EA` panels paired with a 1px solid border (`#EDE5D8` or translucent purple `rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Imperial Accent Outlines:** High-honor modules, featured convocations, and selected credentials feature a crisp `1px` border of burnished gold (`#C89620`).
- **Shadow System (Warm Ochre/Umber Cast):**
  - *Resting Level 1 (Cards, Modules):* `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 26, 34, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.03)`
  - *Resting Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Palettes):* `0 4px 16px rgba(30, 26, 34, 0.08), 0 1px 4px rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.04)`
  - *Modal & Drawer Level 3:* `0 12px 36px rgba(30, 26, 34, 0.16), 0 2px 8px rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.06)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius, scaling to `0.5rem` / 8px for modal containers and cards). This deliberate choice reflects the precision of traditional stone lintels, formal parchment certificates, and collegiate yearbooks, rejecting overly playful or consumer-app bubble geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`rounded-sm` / base radius) for crisp, structured definition.
- **Macro Containers (Cards, Modals, Callouts):** `8px` (`rounded-lg`) to soften outer boundaries while maintaining formal integrity.
- **Emblematic / Insignia Elements:** Circular (`rounded-full`) exclusively for society seals, heraldic crests, and portrait avatars.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Imperial:** Solid Deep Royal Purple (`#3B1259`) background, white (`#FFFFFF`) text, 4px border radius. On hover: transitions to `#4A154B` with a subtle amber outline glow (`0 0 0 1px #C89620`).
- **Secondary Distinction (Gold):** Rich Warm Gold (`#C89620`) background with Deep Slate text (`#1E1A22`). Reserved for action items of honor (e.g., "Admissions Open", "Endow Scholarship").
- **Institutional Outline:** Transparent background, 1px border of `#3B1259`, text `#3B1259`. On hover: surface tints to `rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Plain text with `label-md` styling, accompanied by a subtle right caret or rule underline.

### Cards & Heritage Panels
- Rendered on pure parchment surfaces with 1px hairline borders (`#EDE5D8`).
- **Heraldic Header Style:** Optional top border accent in solid burnished gold (`3px` height, `#C89620`) for certified notices, governing board announcements, and accreditation records.
- Generous internal padding (`space-xl`), keeping content unhurried and dignified.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Surface:** Parchment neutral (`#FFFFFF` or `#FCFAF7`), framed by a 1px border of `#D4C9BC`.
- **Typography:** `body-md` in Inter.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to Royal Purple (`#3B1259`) with a soft gold ring offset (`0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 150, 32, 0.18)`).
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` uppercase with deep slate tone `#4A4350`.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- **Heritage Badge:** Deep purple background (`rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.08)`), imperial purple text (`#3B1259`), framed by a fine border (`rgba(59, 18, 89, 0.15)`).
- **Merit / Honor Tag:** Light amber ground (`rgba(200, 150, 32, 0.12)`), rich gold text (`#9A6F0D`), indicating distinctions, scholarships, and academic terms.
- **Geometry:** Rectangular with soft 4px corners; never circular pill-shaped.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square checkbox (4px radius) and circle radio button with a `1.5px` stroke of `#4A4350`.
- Checked state fills with Royal Purple (`#3B1259`) and reveals a gold checkmark or inner pip (`#C89620`).

### Specialized Society Components
- **The Society Crest Seal:** An SVG emblem container framed with concentric hairline circles and gold leaf detailing.
- **Honor Roll & Memorial Table:** Tabular records styled with alternating vellum rows, brass-accented column dividers, and Playfair serif metrics.
- **Charter Notice Banner:** Pinned global notification with Deep Imperial Purple ground, gold heraldic typography, and a centered formal insignia.
